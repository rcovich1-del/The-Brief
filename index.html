Bull put spread

What this screen looks for: stocks and ETFs priced $20 to $500, above their 50-day average, with options expensive for them (IV Rank over 40, IV Percentile over 50) and heavy trading.

What it is: Sell a put and buy a lower-strike put with the same expiration, collecting a credit. It keeps the credit if the stock stays above the sold strike at expiration.

Main risk: If the stock falls below the bought put, the loss is the gap between the two strikes minus the credit, which is usually several times the credit.
