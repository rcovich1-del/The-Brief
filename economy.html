  • Bonds and interest rates

    Both curve spreads in section 03 are positive: 10y−2y at +0.33 pp and 10y−3m at +0.89 pp. Effective fed funds sits at 3.63% below the 4.56% two-year and 4.95% ten-year in the same table.     Section 10 shows the broad trade-weighted dollar index at 118.07 and the advanced-economies index at 111.74 (4 Sep 2026), with UUP +3.85% year to date, EUR/USD at 1.1602 and USD/JPY at 153.54 (11 Sep 2026).
